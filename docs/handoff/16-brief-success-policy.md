# Mission Brief success criteria

**Proposed consumer profile v1.** In the local simulation, choose **Add success criteria** in New mission or Edit brief. The preview states the rule before save. Open the mission’s Brief inspector and choose **Inspect fixture attempt A**, **Inspect corrected candidate B**, then **Reveal B’s matching evidence**. With the initial rule, A earns full rubric marks but fails independent review. B waits for its own observation, then earns a weighted score above the threshold. Protected acceptance remains pending. The controls inspect synthetic observations. They perform no external work.

Each attempt card identifies its policy, artifact digest, observation digest, evaluator and method under **Evaluation provenance**. **Replay this point** rewinds the Project timeline to the recorded event. **Live** returns to the current simulation. Reload restores the traces of Projects with a fixture Brief in the same browser tab. Applying a changed rule creates a fresh local revision. Earlier cards still display the rule and result that applied at the time. Existing Briefs and templates can omit the profile.

## Fixture evaluation and replay

The rubric grades the mission candidate directly. Accuracy and clarity each have named anchors at zero, fifty and one hundred. The weighted mean is the sum of each score multiplied by its weight divided by the total weight. Comparison uses the unrounded score. Each configured floor must also pass. Independent review, artifact checks and the optional synthetic verified-example count are separate gates. Free-text requirements have unknown evidence until an observation source exists for them. An omitted optional gate adds no requirement.

A and B are fixed local artifacts. A has two synthetic verified examples and B has four. Changing a target changes the rule used to evaluate those observations. A failed gate can request a revision only within the remaining attempt, cost, time and UTC deadline limits. Cancellation, uncertain effects and denied, expired, revoked or uncertain authorization stop the fixture. The fixture has no control to retry a permission request or commit acceptance.

New mission and Edit brief both pass through `normBrief`. The simulation fold reads `mission.opened` and `mission.briefed` to install the current Brief. A `brief.fixture` event carries an attempt snapshot with its rule and evidence binding. The fold validates it against the expected next fixture step before adding it to history. A `brief.hold` records a stop that survives later edits. Seeking reconstructs the same history up to the cursor. The inspector displays both the historical evaluation and the current next step.
